Privacy policy

Application: rclone personal backup. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Who uses it

The application is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive, in order to keep a backup copy of the owner's files.

What is collected and shared

No data is collected by the developer, stored on any server of the developer, sold, or shared with third parties. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs is limited to providing the backup function described above, for the owner only.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account security settings, section "Third-party apps with account access".
